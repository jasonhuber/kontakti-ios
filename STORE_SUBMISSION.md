### Description

Kontakti is personal relationship intelligence for the people you genuinely
want to stay connected with. It keeps names, conversations, notes, follow-ups,
and relationship context together without turning your life into a sales CRM.

Use Kontakti to:

- Keep personal and professional contacts organized.
- Log conversations, notes, photos, and voice memos.
- Remember birthdays, contact preferences, and important context.
- See who is overdue for a thoughtful check-in.
- Turn conversations into follow-up tasks.
- Track relationship fitness, weekly goals, and progress.
- Import contacts from your phone and optional Google accounts.
- Keep changes available across iPhone, Android, and the web.

Your data is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Google connections
are optional and are linked only after you create a Kontakti account. You can
delete your account and associated server data from Settings at any time.

## App Review

- Kontakti requires an account because contacts and relationship history sync
  across platforms.
- Reviewer email: `app-review-2026@kontakti.app`
- Reviewer password: retrieve from macOS Keychain service
  `Kontakti Store Reviewer Password`.
- Google linking is optional and appears after sign-in in Settings.
- Account deletion: Settings -> Account -> Delete account, then type `DELETE`.
- Privacy, Terms, and Support are linked from Settings without requiring login.
- The share extension accepts a URL or selected text. The widget shows the Today
  relationship summary after the reviewer signs in to the main app.

## App Privacy worksheet

The app does not track users and does not use data for third-party advertising,
developer advertising, or advertising measurement.

| Data type | Collected | Linked to identity | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Name | Yes | Yes | Account and app functionality |
| Email address | Yes | Yes | Account, authentication, and optional Google sync |
| Phone number | Optional | Yes | Contact management |
| Physical address | Optional | Yes | Contact management |
| Contacts | Optional | Yes | User-requested import and relationship management |
| Emails or text messages | Optional | Yes | User-requested Gmail context and conversation history |
| Photos or videos | Optional | Yes | Contact photos and attachments |
| Audio data | Optional | Yes | Voice memo transcription |
| Other user content | Optional | Yes | Notes, discussions, tasks, and relationship context |
| User ID | Yes | Yes | Authentication and cross-device sync |

All listed data is used for app functionality. Data is encrypted in transit.
Users can request deletion in-app and at
https://kontakti.app/delete-account.html.
